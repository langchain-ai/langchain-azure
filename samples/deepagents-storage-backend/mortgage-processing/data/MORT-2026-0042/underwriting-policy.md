# Demonstration Underwriting Policy

Use only verified packet evidence. Do not infer missing facts.

- Maximum loan-to-value ratio: 80%.
- Maximum debt-to-income ratio: 43%.
- Minimum verified liquid assets: down payment plus $15,000 reserves.
- Declared and verified annual income may differ by up to 5%.
- The appraisal must support the lower of purchase price or appraised value.
- A homeowners insurance binder is required before final approval.
- If a required document is missing but numeric thresholds pass, return `CONDITIONAL APPROVAL` and name the condition.
- Cite every decision input with its `/source/` path.
